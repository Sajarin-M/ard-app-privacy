ARD App

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

This policy explains how ARD App, developed by Sajarin M, handles information when FPS owners use the Android application. ARD App is an independent tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Government of Kerala.

Information used by the app

  • Account and access: An administrator creates accounts. Email addresses, authentication records, account identifiers, FPS assignments, and access permissions are processed by Supabase so that users can sign in and see their assigned shops. The app does not offer public registration. Supabase handles passwords; ARD App does not store plaintext passwords in its own database.
  • Government reports: The app requests stock, transaction, ration-card, and related reports directly from the Kerala ePoS portal. Requests can include district, office, FPS, date, and ration-card identifiers. The portal receives these requests and the device's network address. Report contents are displayed and analyzed on the device; ARD App does not maintain a separate server copy of beneficiary reports.
  • On-device storage: The app saves the sign-in session, access status, preferences, ration-card search history, and selected monthly reports on the device. Ration-card details and pending-card scan results are kept in memory rather than saved as report caches. Signing out clears the session, access status, and saved report cache; preferences and search history can remain on that device. Users can clear search history in the app. Uninstalling the app removes its local data.
  • Diagnostics: The release app uses Sentry to receive JavaScript errors and report-processing failures. Events can contain error messages, device context, breadcrumbs, and report request details such as FPS and ration-card identifiers. Sentry may process the device's network address. The app does not intentionally attach complete report contents or set a Sentry user identity. Session replay and performance tracing are disabled.

How information is used and shared

Account information is used to authenticate users and limit app access to assigned FPSs. Report requests are used to show and analyze the selected ePoS data. Diagnostic events are used to identify and fix failures. The services involved are Supabase for authentication and access records, Kerala ePoS for report requests, and Sentry for diagnostics. ARD App does not use advertising SDKs or sell personal data.

This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log a visitor's network address for site security when the page is opened.

Security, retention, and deletion

Network requests use HTTPS. Access to Supabase records is restricted to the signed-in user's account and assigned FPSs. Report caches and saved sessions remain on the device until they are cleared as described above. Account and assignment records remain in Supabase while access is needed; diagnostic records remain subject to Sentry's configured retention settings.

To request deletion of an ARD App account and its associated access records, email sajarinm@gmail.com. We will verify the request before deleting the account. Deleting an ARD App account does not alter records held by the Kerala ePoS portal. Locally saved preferences and search history can be removed by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.

Children

ARD App is intended for FPS owners and is not directed to children.

Changes and contact

This policy may change when the app or its data practices change. The updated text and revision date will appear on this page. For privacy questions, contact Sajarin M at sajarinm@gmail.com.
